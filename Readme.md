Awesome Chocolate Sales Analysis
________________________________

**Project Overview**
Developed an end-to-end Sales Analytics solution in Microsoft Power BI using Power Query, Star Schema data modeling, and DAX. Built seven interactive dashboards to analyze sales trends, orders, deliveries, product and salesperson performance, and Time Intelligence (MTD, QTD, YTD & YoY Growth%) for data-driven decision-making.

**Core Focus**
Data Cleaning & Transformation, ETL with Power Query, Star Schema Data Modeling, DAX Measure Development, Time Intelligence Analysis, and Interactive Multi-Page Dashboard Design for business reporting and data-driven decision-making.

**Key Highlights**
- Analyzed 13,372 orders across 6 countries and 4 continents (2023–2026)
- Generated 51.09M in Total Revenue with a 13.81% overall Profit Margin
- Achieved a 92.22% Delivered Rate against a 5.61% Cancelled Rate
- Tracked performance of 25 salespeople across 4 sales teams
- Evaluated profitability of 22 chocolate products across 3 categories
- Identified 85% Dark Bars as the top revenue-generating product and Raspberry Choco as the top-selling product
- Identified Team Delish as the top-performing sales team by revenue and profit%
- Built MTD, QTD, and YTD cumulative revenue trend pages with YoY Growth% analysis

**Tools Used**
Microsoft Power BI:
  - Power Query (ETL)
  - Data Modeling (Star Schema)
  - DAX (Measures & Time Intelligence)
  - Interactive Report Pages & Navigation

**Project Workflow**
1. Data Connection —     Imported and connected all datasets (Shipments, Products, Salespeople, Geography) using Power Query.
2. Data Transformation — Cleaned data types, removed duplicates, errors, and nulls; structured tables for modeling.
3. Data Modeling —       Built a data model in Power BI with Shipments_Table as the fact table and SalesPerson, Geography, Product, and Date as dimension tables.
4. Date Table —          Created a dedicated Date_Table using CALENDAR() function, marked as the official date table for time intelligence.
5. DAX Measures —        Written and organized 40+ DAX measures into six folders: Sales Analysis, Order Analysis, Continents, Product Analysis, SalesPerson Analysis, and Time Intelligence.
6. Time Intelligence —   Implemented TOTALMTD, TOTALQTD, TOTALYTD, SamePeriodLastYear, and YoY Growth% measures.
7. Report Pages —        Developed a 7-page interactive report: Executive Overview, Country Analysis, Product Analysis, SalesPerson Analysis, MTD Report, QTD Report, and YTD & Growth Analysis, with slicers for Product Category, Continents, and Sales Team.

**Key Results**
- Generated 51.09M in Total Revenue across 13,372 orders (2023–2026)
- Achieved a 13.81% overall Profit Margin
- Maintained a 92.22% Delivered Rate with a 5.61% Cancelled Rate
- Identified 85% Dark Bars as the top revenue-gaining product (8.92M)
- Identified Raspberry Choco as the top-selling product by order quantity
- Determined Team Delish as the top-performing sales team (15.52M revenue, 13.90% Profit%)
- Analyzed performance across 6 countries, 4 continents, 25 salespeople, and 22 products
